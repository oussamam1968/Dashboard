How to read these figures. “People reached” counts direct participants in interpersonal EORE sessions only. Indirect (media) reach is an estimate and is shown separately, never added to people reached. People trained in Training of Facilitators and Community Focal Point trainings are also counted separately. Only approved reports are included. A session can cover several hazard types, so hazard counts can add up to more than the number of sessions. Group counts are as reported by teams. Map locations are approximate district points.
